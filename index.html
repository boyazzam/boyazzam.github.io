🌐 What Is NetPilot?

NetPilot gives you a simple set of controls over how your devices use the internet. On your Android phone, the app lets you choose which DNS service your connections use, set speed limits so one app cannot swallow all your bandwidth, and watch your traffic in real time. On Windows, a companion program mirrors those ideas on your desktop so the two devices can work together.

The most popular feature is phone-tunnel sharing. Your Android phone can create a private tunnel, and the NetPilot Windows companion can route your computer's internet traffic through that same tunnel. That means your laptop uses the same protected connection your phone is already using. No networking knowledge is required — you pick an option, press start, and NetPilot handles the rest.

✨ Key Features

  • DNS control: Pick a DNS provider from a list or type in your own. Great for faster lookups, ad blocking services, or family-safe filtering.
  • Bandwidth limiter: Cap download and upload speeds per app so video streaming stops choking your video calls.
  • Live traffic monitor: See which apps are sending and receiving data right now, with easy-to-read charts and totals.
  • VPN-based protection: NetPilot uses Android's built-in VPN slot so rules apply to every app without root access.
  • Windows companion: A native desktop app that shows connection status, active rules, and tunnel health in one window.
  • Phone-tunnel sharing: Send your computer's traffic through your phone's tunnel with a quick pairing step.
  • Modern, clean interface: Built with Jetpack Compose on Android and WPF on Windows, so everything is tidy, fast, and easy to read.

📱 Using the Android App

On your phone, open the NetPilot package from the releases page to install it. Android may ask for permission to install apps from this source — allow it once, then continue. When the app opens for the first time, it will ask to set up a VPN connection. This is how NetPilot applies your DNS choices and speed limits to every app. Accept the request and you will land on the main screen.

The main screen has three tabs. DNS lets you choose a provider or enter your own addresses. Limiter lets you set a maximum speed for individual apps. Monitor shows live graphs of what is moving across your connection. Drag the switch at the top to turn everything on or off at once.

🔗 Sharing Your Phone's Tunnel With Your PC

Connect your phone and computer to the same Wi-Fi network first. Open NetPilot on your phone and go to the tunnel section, then tap Share tunnel. A short pairing code appears on the phone screen.

Now open the Windows companion and choose Connect to phone. Type the pairing code from your phone into the box and press confirm. The status light on both devices turns green once the link is live. From that moment on, traffic from your computer travels through the same tunnel as your phone.

To stop sharing, press Disconnect in the Windows companion, then tap Stop sharing on your phone. Closing the apps also ends the link, so nothing stays running in the background.

🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

The Windows program will not open. Right-click the file and choose Run as administrator. If that fails, download the file again from the releases page in case the earlier copy was interrupted.

The phone shows a VPN warning. This is normal. NetPilot needs that slot to apply your DNS and speed rules. You can remove it at any time in Android settings.

The pairing code is rejected. Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, then ask your phone for a fresh code — codes expire after a short time.

Speeds dropped after setting a limit. Open the Limiter tab and raise the cap, or switch the rule off entirely. Limits apply immediately and do not need a restart.
